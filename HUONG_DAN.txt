LUU Y QUAN TRONG VE DB-FIRST (.edmx):
  Project nay dung EF6 Code-First-to-existing-Database (POCO + DbContext
  MyStoreEntities), chay duoc luon voi CSDL MyStore da tao.
  Neu co yeu cau bat buoc phai co MyStoreModel.edmx (DB-First) nhu trong slide:
    Models > Add > New Item > Data > ADO.NET Entity Data Model > ten MyStoreModel
    > EF Designer from database > chon CSDL MyStore > Finish.
    Sau do xoa file Models/MyStoreEntities.cs (vi EDMX se tu sinh Context + POCO).
    Toan bo Controllers/Views khong phai sua (dung cung ten MyStoreEntities).
